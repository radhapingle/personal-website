About

Hi! I'm Radha, a student at Carnegie Mellon studying Cognitive Science with a concentration in Human-Computer Interaction and a minor in Information Systems. I've always been fascinated by human behavior, how our brains shape the way we think, decide, and move through the world. I'm especially interested in how people experience technology, and in using those insights to shape digital tools that feel like they were actually made for someone.
